# Family Tree

A simple, private place to record a family: who the people are, how they are related, and their stories.

## Language

**Tree**:
The record of one family, with all its people and how they are related. One family is one tree. Every tree is stored on the server, and everyone who can reach the server can see and change it. A server can hold several trees, for example one made for a friend.
_Avoid_: Family, project, workspace, database, Device tree, Synced tree

**Person**:
One individual recorded in a tree, living or dead.
_Avoid_: Member, individual, relative, node

**First names**:
A person's given names, for example "Maria Elena". The only required part of a name.
_Avoid_: Given name, forename, first name (singular)

**Surname**:
A person's current family name.
_Avoid_: Last name, family name

**Birth surname**:
The surname a person had at birth, when it differs from their current one.
_Avoid_: Maiden name, née

**Home person**:
The person the tree view opens on. At first it is the first person added; it can be changed.
_Avoid_: Root person, main person, me

**Story**:
Free text about a person: achievements, memories, anything interesting.
_Avoid_: Description, bio, notes

## Relationships

**Parent link**:
The link from a child to one of their parents. It has a kind: birth, adopted, step or foster.
_Avoid_: Ancestry, lineage, edge

**Partnership**:
Two persons who are or were a couple, married or not. It can have a start (for example a wedding) and an end (for example a divorce). A person can have many partnerships over time.
_Avoid_: Marriage, couple, relationship, spouse link
